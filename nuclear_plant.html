Before you build

EM Control Rods are the exact ceiling. This plant needs ~6.5/min (5 uranium rods + plutonium), and you have ~6/min surplus — you're right at the edge. Nudge EM rod production up a touch, or run the plant at ~90%, and it fits. EM rods, not uranium, are what cap this.
Build it all at the uranium node, sealed off. Everything dashed-green is radioactive — ore, cells, rods, waste, reactors. Nothing radioactive leaves; you export only electricity. Mine uranium locally so it never travels.
Sulfuric Acid is covered. Needs ~75/min; your 2 refineries make 100 — fits with headroom (just get them off 0% by connecting sulfur + water).
Water must be at the node — ~1,500/min (~13 Extractors) for the reactors + blends. You said you can bring plenty, so fine — just confirm the source is there.
Plutonium side is near whole-machine now at 50 Waste/min (1 Non-Fissile Blender, ~0.5 PA, ~1.5 Assembler, 1 Pu Rod Mfr, ~1.25 reactors). Fully consumes the waste; ~12.5 Plutonium Waste/min remains → store it in containers.
