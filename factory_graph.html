How the numbers work

the exact math, no black box

Every rate is items per minute. A recipe lists a quantity per craft and a craft time in seconds, so:

rate = quantity × 60 ÷ craft time (seconds)

Example — a Screw Constructor: 4 screws every 6 s = 4 × 60 ÷ 6 = 40 screws/min. Your 12 of them = 480/min.

For each item I add up everything that makes it and everything that uses it:

supplied = made ÷ needed

Example — Steel Beam: made 60/min, needed 78/min (Versatile Framework 60 + Encased Beam 18). 60 ÷ 78 = 77% supplied → starved.

A line's efficiency is set by its most-starved input (raw ore is assumed always available):

efficiency = the lowest “supplied” among its inputs (capped at 100%)

This is first-order: it uses each input's nameplate output and doesn't chain multiple starvations together. It's honest about that so the number stays easy to reason about.

Power uses the in-game rule that underclocking saves more than linearly:

power = base MW × clock1.6

Your machines are all at 100% clock, so power is just base × machine count. Total: 15003 MW.
