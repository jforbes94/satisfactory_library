Build notes

Drone branch: peel ~1/4 of turbofuel (83/min) off the header into ~2 Packagers → 83 Packaged Turbofuel/min. That's premium drone fuel (dense, fewer refuels). Feed your drone home ports from here.
Empty canisters: packaging needs 83 canisters/min in, and drones leave the empties at the destination ports — loop them back to the Packager or sink them, or the line clogs.
Generators @ ~140% = free power (linear, 1 shard each) — 24 gennies burn the 250 Turbofuel for ~8.3 GW. Add more gennies (or raise the clock) if you later feed them the drone share too.
Consume all 300 Polymer Resin (5 Residual Plastic refineries) or the Fuel refineries clog and everything stops. Bonus 100 Plastic/min toward your shortage.
Split the header with a simple junction — no valve needed. The Packager and generators both pull what they need; if you want to guarantee the 1/4 under contention, a valve on the drone branch caps it, but normally demand sorts it.
