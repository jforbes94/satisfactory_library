Notes on this build

Coal is temporarily short — 229/min vs 400 needed. Keep all the buildings: the Scrap step just throttles to ~57% and everything downstream backpressures to match (machines idle, nothing jams). It auto-recovers to the full 667 ingots the moment coal reaches 400. While starved, the nitrogen tanks hog the scarce ingots, so Alclad/Casing run near-empty until coal's fixed — pause a couple of Packagers to free ingots if you want sheets flowing meanwhile.
Bauxite & water both fit now. 8 refineries at 5/6 clock = exactly 800 Bauxite + 1,200 Water (of your 2,400). Nothing over-drawn.
Whole front half runs at one 5/6 clock. 8 Alumina + 4 Scrap refineries all at 83% — whole machines (no awkward 3.3), and underclocking draws ~90 MW for Scrap vs ~100 at 3.3×100%. Re-clock the line and it all scales together.
No silica factory. The 333 Silica by-product feeds your ~4.4 Standard Foundries; the rest of the scrap goes Pure (Smelter, scrap-only). No Raw Quartz, no silica constructors — the whole branch is gone.
Silica actually feeds ~4.4 Foundries, not 2–3. If you build only 3, ~108 Silica/min goes unused (sink it) and ~130 more Scrap shifts to Pure — ingot total barely changes.
Pure is a Smelter recipe: 2 Scrap → 1 Ingot (no water, no silica). It's less scrap-efficient than Standard (2 vs 1.5 scrap/ingot), which is why 1,200 Scrap yields 667 Ingot, not 800.
Nitrogen eats 225 ingots (as Empty Fluid Tanks), leaving 442 for Alclad + Casing. Split shown is 250 Alclad / 192 Casing — slide it either way (Alclad = 30 ingot +10 copper → 30/min; Casing = 90 ingot → 60/min).
